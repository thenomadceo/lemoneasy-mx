What you would be selling

A push press, two pieces of steel, zero spare parts.

Twenty seconds, no narration. It is the only question that matters: does the product work.

Push, not squeeze

Place half a slice, slide the pusher, and the juice comes out directed, without the big seeds and without touching the fruit. One slice gives up to a tablespoon of juice.

AISI 304 stainless steel

European grade 1.4301, high-polish finish. The same steel as professional cutlery and commercial kitchen equipment.

Two pieces, sealed at the factory

The two pieces are crimped together. It does not come apart, does not lose parts, and generates no spare-part claims.

Dishwasher safe

Rinse it under the tap after use and run it through the dishwasher daily. Built for bar and restaurant service as much as for the home.

Works with lemons and limes

Slices up to 3/8 inch (1 cm) for the standard model. The large model takes quarters. Tested with Persian limes as well as lemons.

Barcoded and retail ready

EAN-coded product, retail ready. The full technical sheet (dimensions, steel grade, packaging, barcode) comes with the sample.
